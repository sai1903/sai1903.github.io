02

Why one spine matters

The integration you don’t have to build

A resignation in HRMS emits one event. Chat revokes channel access, Mail moves the mailbox to legal hold, Admin removes the SSO assignment and the audit log records all three with the same correlation id. That is the whole pitch.

Identity

SSO, SCIM, SCIM again

SAML and OIDC for login, SCIM for provisioning both ways. Bring Okta, Entra or nothing at all.

Data

Residency you choose

Tenant data pinned to India, the EU or the US. Backups stay in region, including the ones nobody thinks about.

Access

Roles, not checkboxes

Permissions derive from position and reporting line, so a promotion does not need a ticket to IT.

Extend

REST, webhooks, exports

Every object readable over the API. Bulk export in CSV and Parquet because leaving should be possible.
