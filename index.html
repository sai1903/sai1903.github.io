05

Doctoral research

Quantum × AGI × ASI

The scaling story that got us here is mostly a story about compute and data. My research asks a narrower question: is there a class of representation that a quantum substrate makes cheap and a classical one does not — and would a reasoning system actually benefit from it?

“Most quantum-for-AI claims collapse once you write down the honest classical baseline. I want to find the few that survive it.”
01

Quantum kernels under an honest baseline

Benchmarking quantum kernel methods against well-tuned classical kernels on the same budget, rather than against a strawman.

02

Hybrid inference for reasoning models

Where a variational circuit could sit inside an otherwise classical stack without the data-loading cost eating the benefit.

03

Evaluation for systems past human ceiling

If ASI-class behaviour is the target, the harder problem is measurement. What does a credible eval look like when you cannot grade the answer?
